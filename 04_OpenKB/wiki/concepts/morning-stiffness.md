# Morning Stiffness

[[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0012]]
[[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0015]]

## Definition
Morning stiffness is a symptom in which joint stiffness is most noticeable after waking and improves gradually with movement. It is a key clinical clue for distinguishing **inflammatory arthritis** from **degenerative joint disease**.

## Typical patterns
- Usually worst in the morning
- Commonly lasts **more than 30 minutes**
- In inflammatory arthritis, it may last **1 hour or longer**
- In some cases, it can persist all day
- Often occurs with joint pain, swelling, and tenderness

## Clinical significance
Morning stiffness is especially important in evaluating arthritis because its duration and associated features help suggest the underlying cause.

- **Prolonged stiffness** supports an **inflammatory** cause
- **Shorter stiffness** is more typical of **degenerative** disease such as [[concepts/osteoarthritis|osteoarthritis]]
- In [[concepts/rheumatoid-arthritis|rheumatoid arthritis]], morning stiffness is a hallmark symptom and often accompanies symmetric involvement of the hands, wrists, and other small joints

## Comparison across arthritis types
### In [[concepts/osteoarthritis|osteoarthritis]]
- Morning stiffness is usually **less than 30 minutes**
- Symptoms are often **localized** and related to joint use
- More common in **older adults**
- Joint involvement is often **asymmetric** and affects **weight-bearing joints**

### In [[concepts/rheumatoid-arthritis|rheumatoid arthritis]]
- Morning stiffness is usually **1 hour or longer**
- Reflects **active inflammatory synovitis**
- Often accompanied by **symmetric** small-joint involvement
- May occur with **RF positivity** and elevated **ESR/CRP**

## Summary
Morning stiffness refers to stiffness after waking that improves with movement. When it lasts longer than 30 minutes, especially 1 hour or more, it strongly suggests an inflammatory arthritis such as [[concepts/rheumatoid-arthritis|rheumatoid arthritis]], whereas short-lived stiffness is more consistent with [[concepts/osteoarthritis|osteoarthritis]].
