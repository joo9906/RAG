# Official Approval Details Used to Identify and Use Herbal Medicines Safely

Official approval details are the officially published product information for a herbal-based or herbal-derived medicine. They include the product’s **ingredient composition, classification, dosage form, approved indications, dosing, price, warnings, and reimbursement-related rules**. In practice, these details are the primary reference for understanding how a product is regulated, how it should be used clinically, and whether use falls within reimbursable scope.

This concept is illustrated by [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0019]], which summarizes the approval information for **Joins Tablet**, by [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0022]], which explains the reimbursement rule for Joins, by [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0035]], which shows how later indication revision and reimbursement reevaluation narrowed a product’s covered use, and by [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0036]], which directly compares **Joins** and **Imotton** to show how approval status, evidence, and coverage differ across products.

## What official approval details usually include

- **Product category**: prescription or nonprescription status
- **Active ingredients and strength**
- **Manufacturer / seller**
- **Regulatory classification**
- **Dosage form and appearance**
- **Price / reimbursement-related information**
- **Approved indications**
- **Dosage and administration**
- **Use-with-caution populations and warnings**
- **Coverage rule within approved indications**

## Why these details matter

Official approval details matter because they connect a product’s **botanical or traditional composition** with its **regulated medical use**. Even when a medicine is herbal or plant-derived, it is still treated as a regulated drug with defined indications, dosing, safety considerations, and reimbursement boundaries.

This makes the concept useful in two main ways:

- **Clinical safety**: determines what the product is approved for and how it should be used.
- **Administrative review**: determines whether use is within the approved scope for reimbursement.

It is also important for understanding how a product’s market position can change when labeling or reimbursement policies are revised.

## Example: Joins Tablet approval information

The Joins materials show how official approval details are used in practice.

### Product approval information from [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0019]]

- **Prescription drug**
- **Ingredient**: *Willow vine, Trichosanthes root, and Prunella herb* 30% ethanol dried extract (40→1), 200 mg
- **Manufacturer**: SK Chemicals Co., Ltd.
- **MFDS classification**: 114, [[concepts/analgesics-anti-inflammatory-drugs|antipyretic, analgesic, anti-inflammatory agents]]
- **Dosage form**: oval yellow-brown film-coated tablet
- **Indications**: symptom relief of **osteoarthritis** and **rheumatoid arthritis**
- **Dosage**: 1 tablet, 3 times daily, orally
- **Caution groups**: patients with infection, pregnant women, possible pregnancy, and nursing mothers

### Reimbursement rule from [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0022]]

- No separate reimbursement standard is specified for Joins
- Reimbursement is recognized when the drug is used **within the approved indication scope**
- This shows that approval status and coverage status are linked through the **authorized use range**

## Example: Joins vs. Imotton comparison

The later comparison document, [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0036]], expands the concept by contrasting a prescription herbal drug with an OTC product.

### Joins tablet in the comparison

- **Composition**: 30% ethanol dried extract of *Clematis mandshurica, Trichosanthes kirilowii root, and Prunella vulgaris* (40→1), 200 mg
- **Classification**: **114** ([[concepts/analgesics-anti-inflammatory-drugs|antipyretic, analgesic, anti-inflammatory agents]])
- **Indications**: symptom relief of **osteoarthritis** and **rheumatoid arthritis**
- **Dose**: **1 tablet three times daily**
- **Price**: **390 KRW per tablet** / **1,170 KRW per day**
- **Evidence note**: Korean natural-product drug with **clinical data suggesting cartilage-protective effects in knee osteoarthritis**
- **Status**: **prescription drug**

### Imotton capsule in the comparison

- **Composition**: avocado-soya unsaponifiables extract, **300 mg**
- **Classification**: **399** ([[concepts/reimbursement-criteria|other metabolic drugs]])
- **Indications**: symptom relief of **adult knee osteoarthritis**
- **Dose**: **1 capsule once daily**, taken **with meals**
- **Price**: **376 KRW per capsule** / **376 KRW per day**
- **Evidence note**: no convincing cartilage-protective clinical evidence; clinical usefulness described as uncertain
- **Status**: **over-the-counter drug**
- **Coverage note**: maintained under **conditional reimbursement** based on cost-effectiveness

## How labeling changes affect use and coverage

[[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0035]] shows that official approval details are not static.

- The product discussed there, **Iimotun** (avocado-soya unsaponifiables), underwent **reimbursement appropriateness review**
- Although it was judged to have **no reimbursement appropriateness**, coverage was still retained under **conditional coverage**
- The approved indication scope was revised in **2021.07** after the origin country, France, changed the label for the reference product **Piascledine®**
- As a result, the Korean indication was narrowed to:
  - **Symptom relief of adult knee osteoarthritis**
- Earlier indications had been broader and included:
  - **Osteoarthritis (degenerative osteoarthritis)**
  - **Adjunctive treatment for bleeding and pain caused by periodontal disease**

This case is useful because it shows that changing official approval information can directly affect **reimbursement status**, **clinical use**, and **competitive positioning**. It also highlights the role of [[concepts/reimbursement_evaluation]] and [[concepts/conditional_coverage]] when clinical utility is uncertain.

## Key pattern across the documents

Across the Joins and Iimotun/Imotton materials, official approval details serve as the bridge between:

- **What the product is made of**
- **What it is approved to treat**
- **How it should be taken**
- **Who should use caution**
- **Whether insurance coverage applies**
- **Whether later label revisions narrow or expand real-world use**

The Joins comparison in [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0036]] also shows an important distinction between products with broader prescription-based musculoskeletal use and products with narrower OTC labeling and less certain evidence.

## Practical takeaway

When reviewing a herbal medicine, official approval details are the starting point for understanding **what the product is, what it is approved for, how it should be taken, who needs extra caution, and whether its use falls within reimbursable scope**. The Joins and Imotton examples show that these details can also change over time, affecting both clinical positioning and insurance coverage.
