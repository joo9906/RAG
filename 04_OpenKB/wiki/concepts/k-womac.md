# K-WOMAC

**K-WOMAC** stands for the **Korean Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index**. It is a standardized assessment tool used to evaluate symptoms and functional status in patients with **knee osteoarthritis**.

## What it measures

K-WOMAC is used to assess three main domains:

- **Pain**
- **Stiffness**
- **Physical function**

Because it captures both symptom severity and daily functional impact, K-WOMAC is commonly used in osteoarthritis clinical research and treatment evaluation.

## Why it matters

In osteoarthritis studies, K-WOMAC provides a structured way to measure whether a treatment improves a patient's ability to move and perform daily activities, not just whether pain changes.
