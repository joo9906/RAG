# dGEMRIC

## Definition
**dGEMRIC** stands for **Delayed Gadolinium-Enhanced Magnetic Resonance Imaging of Cartilage**. It is an MRI-based method used to evaluate the **glycosaminoglycan (GAG) content** of cartilage, which reflects cartilage matrix composition and health.

## What It Measures
- Assesses **GAG concentration** in articular cartilage
- Serves as an indirect indicator of **proteoglycan integrity**
- Helps evaluate early cartilage biochemical changes, sometimes before major structural damage is visible

## Why It Matters
Because GAG is a major component of cartilage proteoglycans, dGEMRIC is useful for studying whether a treatment helps **preserve cartilage composition**. A maintained dGEMRIC index suggests better cartilage biochemical status, while a decrease suggests loss of GAG content.

## Use in the JOINS Document
In [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0031]], dGEMRIC was used in a 1-year osteoarthritis clinical study to compare JOINS® with placebo. The summary reports that:
- the **JOINS® group maintained dGEMRIC index** over 1 year
- the **placebo group showed a significant decrease**
- the change between groups supported a **cartilage-protective effect**

This makes dGEMRIC an important endpoint for assessing [[concepts/cartilage-protection]] in [[concepts/osteoarthritis]] research.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/gag]] — the cartilage component directly evaluated by dGEMRIC
- [[concepts/cartilage-protection]] — therapeutic effect suggested by stable dGEMRIC results

## Notes
- dGEMRIC is a **biomarker-oriented imaging technique**, not a symptom score.
- It is especially valuable for **longitudinal studies** tracking cartilage composition over time.
