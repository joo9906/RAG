# Spinal Osteoarthritis

## Definition
Spinal osteoarthritis is a **degenerative joint condition of the spine**. It is also commonly referred to as **spondylosis**. The condition involves gradual wear and structural change in spinal components such as the vertebrae, intervertebral discs, cartilage, and ligaments.

## Core Features
Spinal osteoarthritis is primarily associated with **age-related degeneration**. As the spine undergoes progressive wear, patients may develop:
- **Low back pain**
- **Stiffness** or reduced flexibility
- **No symptoms** in some cases

In some patients, degenerative changes can lead to **spinal stenosis**, where the spinal canal becomes narrowed and compresses the spinal cord or nerve roots. This may produce:
- Numbness
- Tingling
- Muscle weakness

## Diagnosis
Diagnosis is typically based on a combination of:
- Medical history
- Physical examination
- Imaging studies

Common imaging tools include:
- **X-ray**: to evaluate arthritis and bone damage
- **MRI**: to assess the spinal cord and nerve compression
- **CT**: to examine the spinal canal, bones, and joints

## Treatment
Treatment aims to **reduce pain, improve function, and enhance quality of life**.

### Non-pharmacologic management
- Exercise
- Weight reduction
- Massage
- Heat therapy / warm compresses

### Pharmacologic management
- Acetaminophen
- NSAIDs
- Topical ointments and creams
- Mild narcotics
- Steroid injections

### Surgical treatment
Surgery may be considered when symptoms are severe or when nerve compression becomes clinically significant.
