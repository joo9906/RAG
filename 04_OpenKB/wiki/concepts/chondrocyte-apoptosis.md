# Chondrocyte Apoptosis

**Chondrocyte apoptosis** is the programmed death of **chondrocytes**, the cells responsible for producing and maintaining cartilage. When too many chondrocytes undergo apoptosis, cartilage repair and maintenance decline, accelerating tissue degeneration.

## Why it matters

Chondrocytes are essential for preserving cartilage structure and function. Their survival supports:

- cartilage matrix production
- tissue maintenance and repair
- resistance to degeneration in joint disease

Excessive chondrocyte apoptosis weakens these functions and is associated with cartilage breakdown in degenerative joint conditions such as osteoarthritis.
