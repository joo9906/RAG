# Extra-Articular Manifestations

[[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0012]] describes [[concepts/rheumatoid-arthritis|rheumatoid arthritis (RA)]] as a disease that can involve more than just the joints. **Extra-articular manifestations** are symptoms or complications that occur in organs and tissues outside the synovial joints.

## What the concept means

In RA, inflammation is not limited to the hands, wrists, feet, and other joints. The disease may extend to other body systems, reflecting its systemic inflammatory nature.

## Examples from the source

The source lists several extra-articular manifestations of RA:

- **Vasculitis** — inflammation of blood vessels
- **Pulmonary fibrosis** — scarring of the lungs
- **Conjunctivitis** — inflammation of the eye surface
- **Pericarditis** — inflammation of the lining around the heart
- **Lymphadenitis** — inflammation of lymph nodes

## Why it matters

Extra-articular manifestations suggest that RA can affect overall health more broadly than joint pain and stiffness alone. Recognizing them is important because they may indicate more severe or widespread disease.
