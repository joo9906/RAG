# Corticosteroids

Corticosteroids are a class of drugs with powerful **anti-inflammatory** and immunosuppressive effects. They are used to quickly reduce inflammation in many conditions, especially when rapid symptom control is needed.

## Role in Rheumatoid Arthritis

In the source document on [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0014]], corticosteroids are listed as part of the medication options for **rheumatoid arthritis (RA)**. Their main role is to provide **strong anti-inflammatory action** and help control active disease symptoms.

### Key points from the source
- Used for their **strong anti-inflammatory effect**
- Helpful in reducing inflammatory activity in RA
- Serve as part of broader RA drug management alongside [[concepts/NSAIDs]], [[concepts/DMARDs]], and [[concepts/biologic-therapy]]

## Why they matter

Corticosteroids are often used when inflammation must be controlled quickly. In RA, they do not primarily define long-term disease modification like [[concepts/DMARDs]], but they are important for short-term suppression of inflammation and symptom relief.
