# Symptomatic vs Disease-Modifying OA Drugs

## Overview
In osteoarthritis (OA), therapies are commonly grouped by whether they **relieve symptoms** or **modify the underlying disease process**. Across the source documents, most currently used OA treatments are **symptomatic**: they reduce pain, improve function, and support daily activity, but do not reverse the structural degeneration of OA.

This practical framework is reinforced by the **2019 ACR guideline** for hand, knee, and hip OA, which emphasizes a stepped approach with strongly recommended and conditionally recommended options such as **exercise**, **weight loss**, **NSAIDs**, and **intra-articular (IA) steroid injections** [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0010]]. These options are primarily used to reduce pain and improve function, not to cure OA.

The source documents provide several concrete examples of symptom-directed OA drugs, especially **Joins Tablet®** and **JOINS®**. Joins Tablet® is indicated for **osteoarthritis symptom relief** and is dosed orally for pain and inflammation control, not for structural reversal [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0019]]. JOINS® was also tested in a knee OA randomized controlled trial and showed **similar short-term efficacy** to diclofenac with **better safety and tolerability**, supporting its role as a symptom-relief drug rather than a proven disease-modifying agent [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0024]].

Later studies add nuance. A placebo-controlled trial using **MRI-based structural biomarkers** over **12 months + 12-month extension** in OA patients (n=69) reported a **superior cartilage-protective effect** for JOINS® versus placebo [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0030]]. Another clinical study found that after **1 year of JOINS® use**, patients had significant improvements in **VAS** and **K-WOMAC** compared with placebo, indicating better pain control and joint function [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0033]]. A separate comparison document also notes Joins as a **prescription medicine** with evidence for **knee OA, spinal arthritis**, and **rheumatoid arthritis (RA)** symptom relief, while its comparator **Imotton** is an **OTC medicine** limited to **knee OA** and described as having uncertain clinical utility despite reimbursement support based on cost-effectiveness [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0036]]. The newest source refines this contrast: Imotton’s label is limited to **adult knee OA symptom relief**, it has **conditional reimbursement**, and it lacks evidence for cartilage protection, while JOINS continues to be discussed as having broader evidence signals beyond pure symptom control [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0037]]. Together, these findings suggest that JOINS has signals beyond pure symptom relief, but the overall source material still places it mainly within the symptomatic treatment landscape because disease modification has not been broadly established in routine OA care.

## Core distinction
### Symptomatic OA drugs
Symptomatic drugs are used to reduce the signs and symptoms of OA, especially:

- pain
- inflammation
- stiffness
- functional limitation

They can help patients feel better and move more easily, but they do **not** reliably stop the underlying progression of joint degeneration.

### Disease-modifying OA drugs (DMOADs)
DMOADs are intended to **alter the course of OA** by reducing, slowing, or preventing structural joint damage.

In principle, a true DMOAD would:

- preserve cartilage or joint structure
- slow disease progression
- reduce long-term joint deterioration

However, the source material indicates that such disease-modifying effects have **not been clearly proven** for existing OA medications in routine clinical use, even though individual trials may show promising structural signals.

## Categories described in the source documents
The documents divide OA medications into three practical groups:

### SYRADOA
**Symptomatic Rapid Acting Drugs in OA**

- acetaminophen
- NSAIDs
- intra-articular corticosteroids

These are used for relatively fast symptom relief and align with the ACR guideline emphasis on **NSAIDs** and **IA steroid injections** as key treatment options in OA management [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0010]].

### SYSADOA
**Symptomatic Slow Acting Drugs in OA**

- glucosamine/chondroitin sulfate
- avocado/soybean unsaponifiables
- diacerin
- Joins®

These agents are aimed primarily at symptom control, often with a slower onset of effect than SYRADOA drugs. The Joins Tablet® approval information shows that it is used for **symptom relief of osteoarthritis** and is dosed as **1 tablet, three times daily orally** [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0019]]. The clinical trial summary for JOINS® further supports this classification by showing **comparable efficacy to diclofenac** over 4 weeks with a favorable safety profile [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0024]].

At the same time, the MRI-based placebo-controlled study suggests a possible **cartilage-protective signal** for JOINS® [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0030]], and the 1-year placebo comparison showed improvements in **VAS** and **K-WOMAC** [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0033]]. The Joins vs. Imotton comparison also places Joins as a **prescription product** with broader OA and RA-related use, in contrast to Imotton's narrower OTC knee OA indication and more uncertain clinical role [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0036]]. The new document adds that Imotton’s indication is specifically **adult knee OA symptom relief**, with **one capsule once daily** and a **cost advantage**, but its guideline support remains uncertain and it lacks cartilage-protection evidence [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0037]]. This makes JOINS particularly interesting as a SYSADOA-like agent with emerging structural and long-term efficacy data, though not enough to reclassify it as a confirmed DMOAD.

### DMOADs
**Disease Modifying OA Drugs**

- drugs with the potential to affect structural progression

The documents present DMOADs as a desired but not yet established class in routine OA care.

## How the ACR guideline fits in
The 2019 ACR guideline for hand, knee, and hip OA emphasizes a practical treatment approach. In the source slide, treatments are grouped into **strongly recommended** and **conditionally recommended** options, including:

- **Exercise**
- **Weight loss**
- **NSAIDs**
- **Intra-articular steroid injections**

This reinforces the idea that current guideline-based OA care is largely **symptom-directed** and **function-focused**, even when it uses medications or injections. See also [[concepts/osteoarthritis-management]], [[concepts/nonpharmacologic-treatment]], [[concepts/NSAIDs]], and [[concepts/intra-articular-corticosteroids]].

## Where Joins® fits
Joins Tablet® provides a useful example of a **symptomatic slow-acting OA drug**:

- **Indication:** osteoarthritis symptom relief and rheumatoid arthritis symptom relief [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0019]]
- **Dose:** 1 tablet orally, 3 times daily
- **Role in OA care:** symptom control, not proven disease modification

The JOINS® RCT adds another important data point:

- **Population:** knee OA patients (n = 249)
- **Comparison:** JOINS 200 mg tid vs diclofenac SR 100 mg qd
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Outcome:** similar clinical efficacy, with better safety and tolerability for JOINS [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0024]]

A later MRI-based trial extends the picture:

- **Population:** OA patients (n = 69)
- **Comparison:** JOINS 200 mg tid vs placebo
- **Duration:** 12 months + 12-month extension
- **Outcome:** superior cartilage-protective effect on MRI biomarkers such as **dGEMRIC** and **subarticular BME** [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0030]]

Another 1-year placebo-controlled study found:

- **Outcome measures:** VAS and K-WOMAC
- **Result:** significant improvement in pain and knee function versus placebo [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0033]]

Finally, the Joins vs. Imotton comparison highlights regulatory and practical differences:

- **Joins:** prescription medicine; broader OA use and RA-related evidence; domestic natural-product drug with cartilage-protective clinical data in knee OA [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0036]]
- **Imotton:** OTC medicine; narrowed to adult knee OA; once-daily with meals; conditional reimbursement; clinical utility remains uncertain despite cost-effectiveness support [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0037]]

Taken together, these sources position Joins® as primarily a **symptom-focused SYSADOA**, while also highlighting emerging evidence that it may influence structural biomarkers and longer-term clinical outcomes in OA.

## Why the distinction matters
This distinction is important because OA management often combines:

- [[concepts/osteoarthritis-management|non-pharmacologic therapy]] such as exercise, weight control, and strengthening
- symptomatic medications for pain relief
- injections for selected patients
- ongoing search for true disease-modifying therapies

Understanding whether a medication is symptomatic or disease-modifying helps clinicians and patients set realistic expectations about treatment outcomes.

## Clinical implication
Most currently used OA therapies are best understood as **supportive and symptom-directed**. They improve quality of life and function, but they do not yet provide a proven cure or structural reversal of OA.

The guideline-based options highlighted in [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0010]]—especially **exercise**, **weight loss**, **NSAIDs**, and **IA steroid injections**—fit into this symptomatic framework, and Joins® [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0019]] is another example of symptom-focused OA pharmacotherapy. The JOINS® comparative trial further shows that even when symptom relief is comparable to a standard NSAID, the main demonstrated advantage may be **tolerability and safety**, not disease modification [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0024]]. The newer placebo-controlled MRI study and 1-year clinical study add promising structural and functional signals, and the Joins vs. Imotton comparison adds regulatory context, but these remain individual findings within a broader field where DMOAD status is still unconfirmed [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0030]] [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0033]] [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0036]] [[summaries/2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0037]].
