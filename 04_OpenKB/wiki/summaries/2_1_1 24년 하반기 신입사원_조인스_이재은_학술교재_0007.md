# Summary: 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0007

## Overview
This document provides a concise overview of the representative symptoms of osteoarthritis (OA) and the typical pattern of functional limitation associated with the disease.

## Key points
- **OA pain is activity-related**: joint pain is a core symptom, especially when the joint is used or during movement.
- **Morning stiffness is usually brief**: stiffness is typically present, but the document notes **morning stiffness under 30 minutes**.
- **Range of motion decreases**: affected joints may have limited mobility.
- **Swelling may occur**: joint edema is listed as a common symptom.
- **Joint noises can be present**: audible joint sounds are included among the characteristic findings.
- **Structural changes may develop**: joint deformity is noted as a symptom/manifestation of progression.

## Clinical significance
The document emphasizes a symptom cluster that helps distinguish OA from other joint disorders: pain with activity, short-duration stiffness, and mechanical signs such as decreased motion and joint sounds. These features are closely related to [[concepts/osteoarthritis]] and broader concepts of [[concepts/joint pain]] and [[concepts/joint stiffness]].

## Takeaway
OA is presented as a degenerative joint condition with pain during activity, brief morning stiffness, reduced mobility, swelling, crepitus, and possible deformity.
