# Summary: 조인스 OA 임상3상 VAS 결과

## Overview
This document summarizes the osteoarthritis (OA) phase 3 clinical trial result comparing **JOINS** with **diclofenac** using the **VAS (Visual Analogue Scale)** pain score.

## Key Finding
- In OA patients, **JOINS® showed analgesic efficacy equivalent to diclofenac**.
- After 4 weeks, both groups showed a reduction in pain.
- The between-group difference was **not statistically significant**.

## Trial Result Details
- **JOINS group**: VAS change = **-14.18 ± 17.53 mm**
- **Diclofenac group**: VAS change = **-15.49 ± 15.37 mm**
- **Group comparison**: **p = 0.53**

## Interpretation
- The similar reduction in VAS scores suggests that JOINS may provide **comparable pain relief** to diclofenac in OA patients.
- Because the difference was not significant, the trial supports the view that JOINS has a clinically meaningful analgesic effect in this setting.
