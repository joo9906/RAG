# Summary: 조인스 — Osteoarthritis Causes

## Overview
This document summarizes the causes and risk factors of osteoarthritis (OA), distinguishing between **primary (idiopathic)** and **secondary (acquired)** OA.

## Key Points

### Primary osteoarthritis
- The exact cause is not clearly known.
- Likely influenced by several risk factors:
  - Age
  - Sex
  - Genetic factors
  - Obesity

### Secondary osteoarthritis
- Caused by conditions that damage articular cartilage.
- Common causes include:
  - **Trauma** that injures cartilage
  - **Disease** affecting the joint
  - **Deformity** or structural abnormality
- Representative examples:
  - Joint cartilage destruction after **bacterial arthritis** or **tuberculous arthritis**
  - OA developing after severe impact or repeated minor trauma

## Conceptual Takeaways
- OA can be understood as either a degenerative process with unclear origin or as a consequence of direct cartilage injury.
- [[concepts/risk factors]] such as aging, genetics, and obesity are important in primary OA.
- [[concepts/joint damage]] and [[concepts/cartilage injury]] are central to secondary OA.
- [[concepts/trauma]] and [[concepts/post-infectious joint disease]] may contribute to later joint degeneration.
