# Summary: JOINS RA Phase 3 VAS Results

## Overview
This document summarizes a rheumatoid arthritis (RA) phase 3 clinical trial comparing the analgesic effect of **JOINS** with **celecoxib** using **VAS (visual analogue scale) pain scores**.

## Main Finding
- **JOINS® showed analgesic efficacy equivalent to celecoxib**.
- In both groups, VAS pain scores decreased significantly over time.
- By **Week 6**, there was **no statistically significant difference** between the two groups in VAS change.

## Trial Results
### Group-level VAS change
- **JOINS group (n=87)**
  - Week 3: **-13.64 ± 16.62 mm**
  - Week 6: **-18.38 ± 20.80 mm**
  - Within-group change was significant (**p < 0.001**)

- **Celecoxib group (n=87)**
  - Week 3: **-14.45 ± 15.89 mm**
  - Week 6: **-17.87 ± 19.06 mm**
  - Within-group change was significant (**p < 0.001**)

### Between-group comparison
- Week 6 VAS change difference between the two groups was **not significant** (**p = 0.8662**).

## Interpretation
This trial suggests that JOINS may provide **pain relief comparable to celecoxib** in RA patients, at least as measured by VAS improvement over 6 weeks.
