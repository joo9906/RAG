# Summary: 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0002

## Overview
This document gives a short anatomical explanation of **cartilage (연골)** and relates it to the basic structure of a joint. It emphasizes cartilage as a protective, flexible tissue found at the ends of bones.

## Key Points

### Cartilage characteristics
- Cartilage is fundamentally similar to bone in its role as a structural tissue.
- It has **elasticity**, which helps protect the ends of bones in a joint.
- It does **not contain blood vessels or nerves**.

### Joint-related components shown with cartilage
The document presents cartilage together with the main parts of a joint:
- **Cartilage (연골)**
- **Synovial membrane (활막)**
- **Ligament (인대)**
- **Synovial fluid (활액)**
- **Joint cavity (관절강)**

## Anatomical significance
The document connects cartilage to the broader concept of [[concepts/joint_anatomy|joint anatomy]] by showing how it works alongside synovial membrane, ligaments, and synovial fluid. This highlights cartilage as a cushioning and protective structure in movable joints.
