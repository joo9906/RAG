# Summary: 조인스

This document outlines the key components of cartilage and explains how each contributes to cartilage function.

## Main points

### 1. Collagen
- Cartilage contains collagen, especially **Type II collagen** in articular cartilage.
- Collagen is a fibrous protein and a major component of connective tissue, including skin, ligaments, and bone.
- Its main role in cartilage is to provide **elasticity**.

### 2. Proteoglycan
- Proteoglycans are the main substances that form the cartilage matrix.
- They have a core protein with multiple **glycosaminoglycans (GAGs)** attached.
- Examples of GAGs include:
  - Keratan sulfate
  - Chondroitin sulfate
- Proteoglycans form a mesh-like structure with collagen, helping maintain **mobility** and reducing friction in joints.

### 3. Chondrocyte
- Chondrocytes are cartilage cells.
- They are responsible for the **growth and maintenance** of cartilage.

## Core takeaway
- Cartilage function depends on the interaction of [[concepts/collagen]], [[concepts/proteoglycan]], and [[concepts/chondrocyte]].
- In particular, collagen supports elasticity, proteoglycan supports lubrication and mobility, and chondrocytes maintain cartilage tissue over time.
