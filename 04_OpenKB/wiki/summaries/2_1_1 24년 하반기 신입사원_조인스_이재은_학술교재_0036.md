# Summary: Joins vs. Imotton

This document compares **Joins tablets** and **Imotton capsules** in terms of composition, approved indications, drug classification, pricing, dosing, and the strength of clinical evidence.

## Key comparison

### Joins tablet
- Contains a 30% ethanol dried extract of **Clematis mandshurica, Trichosanthes kirilowii root, and Prunella vulgaris**.
- Classified by the Ministry of Health and Welfare as **114: analgesic/anti-inflammatory**.
- Indicated for symptom relief in **osteoarthritis** and **rheumatoid arthritis**.
- Usual dose: **1 tablet three times daily**.
- Price: **390 KRW per tablet** or **1,170 KRW per day**.
- Described as a Korean natural-product drug with **clinical data suggesting cartilage-protective effects in knee osteoarthritis**.

### Imotton capsule
- Contains **avocado-soy unsaponifiables extract 300 mg**.
- Classified as **399: other metabolic drugs**.
- Indicated for symptom relief in **adult knee osteoarthritis**.
- Usual dose: **1 capsule once daily with meals**.
- Price: **376 KRW per capsule** or **376 KRW per day**.
- Developed in France; the document notes **no convincing cartilage-protective clinical evidence** and describes its clinical utility as uncertain, though it has maintained conditional reimbursement based on cost-effectiveness.

## Prescription-drug vs. over-the-counter distinctions

The document also highlights differences between a **prescription medicine** and an **OTC medicine**:

- **Joins** is a **prescription drug**, so it can be used more broadly in the osteoarthritis spectrum, including **knee osteoarthritis** and **spinal arthritis**.
- It also has clinical data and an approved indication related to **rheumatoid arthritis**.
- **Imotton** is an **OTC drug**, and its indication was narrowed from general osteoarthritis to **knee osteoarthritis only**.
- Because of this narrowed scope, reimbursement is limited to use within the approved range.
- The document mentions a possible **fishy odor reflux** adverse effect, which is why it should be taken **with meals**.

## Main takeaways

- **Joins** is positioned as a broader, prescription-based natural-product therapy with more clinical support for musculoskeletal disease indications.
- **Imotton** is a simpler once-daily OTC option for knee osteoarthritis, but its clinical usefulness is described as less certain.
- The comparison centers on the tension between **regulatory scope, clinical evidence, and reimbursement policy**.
