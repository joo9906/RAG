# Summary: 조인스 RA 임상3상 이상반응 비교

## Overview
This document summarizes adverse event results from a phase 3 rheumatoid arthritis (RA) clinical study comparing **JOINS** and **celecoxib**.

## Key Finding
- The incidence of overall adverse reactions was **similar between JOINS and celecoxib**.
- There was **no statistically significant difference** in either:
  - overall adverse reactions, or
  - drug-related adverse reactions.

## Reported Results
- **JOINS group (n=91)**: adverse reactions in **41.8%**
- **Celecoxib group (n=92)**: adverse reactions in **39.1%**
- **p = 0.7171**

- **Drug-related adverse reactions**:
  - JOINS: **29.7%**
  - Celecoxib: **23.9%**
  - **p = 0.3792**

## Interpretation
The study indicates that JOINS had a safety profile comparable to celecoxib in this RA phase 3 setting. This supports discussion of [[concepts/NSAID safety]], [[concepts/adverse reactions]], and [[concepts/rheumatoid arthritis clinical trials]].

## Reference
- Song YW et al. *Clin Ther.* 2007 May;29(5):862-873.
