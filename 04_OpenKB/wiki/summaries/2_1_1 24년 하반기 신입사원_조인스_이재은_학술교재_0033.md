# Summary: JOINS OA Cartilage Protection Phase 4 Clinical Study

This document summarizes a phase 4 clinical study of **JOINS®** for osteoarthritis (OA), focusing on its effect on **pain relief** and **joint function improvement** over long-term use.

## Main findings

- In the **1-year treatment group**, JOINS® showed **significant improvements** versus placebo in both:
  - **VAS** scores, a pain assessment measure
  - **K-WOMAC** scores, a measure of knee pain, stiffness, and physical function
- The document states that JOINS® demonstrated benefits for both **physical function** and **pain reduction**.

## Key concepts

- **[[concepts/osteoarthritis]]**: The clinical context of the study is knee osteoarthritis.
- **[[concepts/pain-assessment]]**: Pain was evaluated using **VAS (Visual Analogue Scale)**.
- **[[concepts/joint-function-assessment]]**: Joint function was evaluated using **K-WOMAC**.
- **[[concepts/clinical-evidence]]**: The document presents clinical trial evidence supporting JOINS®.

## Measures used

### VAS
- **Visual Analogue Scale**
- Used to assess pain intensity on a **0–100 mm** scale

### K-WOMAC
- **Korean Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index**
- Used to assess:
  - pain
  - stiffness
  - physical function

## Reference

- Kim JI et al. *Knee Surg Relat Res.* 2017 Sep 1;29(3):217-224.

## Takeaway

The document’s central message is that **long-term JOINS® administration may improve both pain and knee function in osteoarthritis compared with placebo**.
