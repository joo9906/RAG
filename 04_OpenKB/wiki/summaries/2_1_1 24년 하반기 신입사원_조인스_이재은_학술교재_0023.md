# Summary: 조인스 OA·RA Clinical Literature Measures

This document is a terminology-focused reference for clinical research related to Joins in osteoarthritis (OA) and rheumatoid arthritis (RA). Its main contribution is to clarify the meaning of common evaluation variables used in study outcomes, especially imaging and patient-reported measures.

## Key evaluation variables

- **VAS (Visual Analog Scale)**
  - A pain measurement scale used to assess the patient’s subjective pain level.
  - Ranges from **0 mm** (no pain) to **100 mm** (worst imaginable pain).

- **K-WOMAC (Korean Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index)**
  - A self-reported questionnaire for assessing joint function in osteoarthritis.
  - Measures **pain, stiffness, and physical function**.
  - Contains **24 items**.

- **Subarticular BME (Bone Marrow Abnormality)**
  - A score for subarticular bone marrow abnormality in the knee, assessed using **WORMS**.
  - Higher BME scores are associated with structural worsening in OA.

- **dGEMRIC (delayed gadolinium-enhanced MRI of cartilage)**
  - An MRI-based technique used to evaluate the **GAG content** of cartilage.

- **WORMS (Whole-Organ Magnetic Resonance Imaging Score)**
  - A whole-knee MRI scoring system used to quantify degenerative changes.
  - Scores range up to **6**, with lower scores indicating a more normal state.

- **GAG (Glycosaminoglycan)**
  - A component of proteoglycans and an important constituent of cartilage.

- **MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)**
  - A diagnostic imaging method that uses magnetic fields and radio waves to produce internal body images.

## Main takeaway

The document functions as a glossary for interpreting OA/RA clinical study endpoints. It connects symptom-based assessment tools such as [[concepts/pain-assessment]] and [[concepts/patient-reported-outcomes]] with imaging-based structural measures such as [[concepts/mri-scoring]], [[concepts/cartilage-biomarkers]], and [[concepts/osteoarthritis-progression]].

## Why this matters

Understanding these variables is essential for reading and comparing clinical studies, because they capture different dimensions of disease:

- **Symptoms**: VAS
- **Function**: K-WOMAC
- **Structural joint damage**: WORMS, BME
- **Cartilage composition**: dGEMRIC, GAG
- **Imaging modality**: MRI
