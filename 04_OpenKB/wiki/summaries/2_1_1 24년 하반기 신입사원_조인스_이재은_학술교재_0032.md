# Summary: 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0032

## Overview
This document summarizes a phase 4 osteoarthritis (OA) cartilage-protection clinical result for [[concepts/JOINS]] showing that 2 years of treatment was associated with preservation of knee structure, particularly by limiting worsening of subarticular bone marrow edema (BME).

## Key Finding
- In the 2-year treatment group, [[concepts/knee osteoarthritis]] structural protection was observed.
- [[concepts/JOINS]] demonstrated a significant difference versus placebo in change in subarticular BME.
- The JOINS® group maintained subarticular BME over 2 years, while the placebo group showed a significant increase.

## What Subarticular BME Means
- **Subarticular BME** stands for **Subarticular Bone Marrow abnormality scores for Entire Knee**.
- It is an MRI-based scoring measure of abnormality beneath the joint surface.
- Higher scores are associated with worsening OA structural damage.

## Clinical Interpretation
- The result supports a **cartilage-protective / structure-preserving effect** for JOINS® over long-term administration.
- By preventing increase in subarticular BME, JOINS® may help slow radiographic or MRI-detectable progression of OA-related joint damage.

## Reference
- Kim JI et al. *Knee Surg Relat Res.* 2017 Sep 1;29(3):217-224.
