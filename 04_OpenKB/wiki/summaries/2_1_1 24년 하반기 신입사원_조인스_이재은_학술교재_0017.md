# Summary: 조인스

## Overview
This document describes the pharmacological mechanism of **Joins** as an anti-inflammatory and analgesic agent. Its main action is presented as modulation of the **arachidonic acid pathway**, specifically through **COX-2 expression inhibition** and **5-LOX activity suppression**, which reduces production of inflammatory mediators such as prostaglandins.

## Key Mechanism
### Anti-inflammatory and analgesic effect
- Joins acts on the **[[concepts/arachidonic-acid-pathway|arachidonic acid pathway]]**.
- It inhibits **COX-2 (cyclooxygenase-2)** expression.
- It also suppresses **5-LOX (5-lipoxygenase)** activity.
- These effects reduce synthesis of **prostaglandins**, which are major inflammatory mediators.
- As a result, the document attributes **anti-inflammatory** and **pain-relieving** effects to Joins.

## Key Concepts
- **COX**: cyclooxygenase
- **LOX**: lipoxygenase
- **Prostaglandins**: inflammatory mediators produced through the arachidonic acid pathway
- **COX-2 inhibition**: reduces inflammation-related prostaglandin production
- **5-LOX inhibition**: lowers leukotriene-related inflammatory signaling

## Reference
- Kim JH et al. *Biol Pharm Bull.* 2005 Sep;28(9):1615-20.

## Takeaway
The document’s central claim is that Joins provides **anti-inflammatory and analgesic benefits** by targeting two major enzymes in eicosanoid biosynthesis: **COX-2** and **5-LOX**. This places it within the broader topic of [[concepts/anti-inflammatory-mechanisms|anti-inflammatory mechanisms]] and [[concepts/cox-lox-inhibition|COX/LOX inhibition]].
