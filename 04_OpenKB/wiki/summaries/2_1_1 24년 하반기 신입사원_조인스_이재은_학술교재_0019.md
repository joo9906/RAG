# 조인스정® Summary

This document summarizes the official approval information for **Joins Tablet** ([[concepts/medicine-approval-info]]), a prescription drug used to relieve symptoms of osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis.

## Core drug information

- **Category:** Prescription drug
- **Active ingredient / content:** *Willow vine, Trichosanthes root, and Prunella herb* 30% ethanol dried extract (40→1), **200 mg**
- **Manufacturer / seller:** SK Chemicals Co., Ltd.
- **MFDS classification:** **114** — [[concepts/analgesics-anti-inflammatory-drugs|antipyretic, analgesic, anti-inflammatory agents]]
- **Dosage form / appearance:** Oval yellow-brown film-coated tablet
- **Price (as of 2021.06):** 390 KRW per tablet

## Indications

Joins Tablet is indicated for:

- Symptom relief of **osteoarthritis** (degenerative joint disease)
- Symptom relief of **rheumatoid arthritis**

## Dosage and administration

- **Standard dose:** 1 tablet per dose, **3 times daily orally**
- The dose may be adjusted appropriately depending on symptoms

## Precautions: use with caution

The document identifies the following groups requiring careful administration ([[concepts/medication-precautions]]):

1. **Patients with infection or suspected infectious causes**
   - Because resistance to infection may be reduced, measures should be taken to suppress progression of infection.
2. **Pregnant women, women who may be pregnant, and nursing mothers**

## Key takeaways

- Joins is a **prescription anti-inflammatory/analgesic herbal-extract tablet** used for chronic joint disease symptom relief.
- The summary provides a concise snapshot of **approval status, composition, price, indication, dosing, and cautionary populations**.
- Special attention is required in **infection-related cases** and during **pregnancy/lactation**.
