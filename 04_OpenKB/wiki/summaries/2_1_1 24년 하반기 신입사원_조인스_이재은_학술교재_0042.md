# Summary

This document explains the reimbursement reevaluation of **Imomton®** and compares it with the **OARSI** guideline position on **avocado-soybean unsaponifiables (ASU)** for knee osteoarthritis.

## Key points

- **Imomton®** was found to have **uncertain clinical utility** in the reimbursement reevaluation.
- The product’s ingredient is **avocado-soybean unsaponifiables (ASU)**, also described as an avocado-soybean extract.
- Its stated indication is **symptom relief in adult knee osteoarthritis**.
- The review concluded that clinical usefulness was unclear, but **cost-effectiveness was considered sufficient for conditional reimbursement retention**.
- A condition was attached: if clinical utility is not demonstrated in textbooks or clinical guidelines within one year, the drug may be excluded from reimbursement.

## OARSI guideline comparison

The document also summarizes how the **Osteoarthritis Research Society International ([[concepts/osteoarthritis]])** handled ASU in its guidelines:

- **2014 guideline**: ASU was rated **Uncertain**.
- **2019 guideline**: ASU was rated **Level 3 (40%–59% in favor)** and **not recommended**.
- The guideline table identifies ASU as a **nutraceutical** with a **conditional** strength of recommendation.

## Interpretation note

The document notes that the term **“Uncertain”** should not necessarily be interpreted as a strict negative judgment or a recommendation against use. Instead, it may imply that treatment can continue when there is a trust-based physician-patient relationship and the patient shows benefit. The text says a revision of the Korean translated guideline is underway to reflect this nuance.

## Why this matters

- Shows the gap that can exist between **health insurance reimbursement decisions** and **international clinical guidelines**.
- Highlights the evolving position of **ASU** in osteoarthritis care.
- Illustrates how recommendation language such as **uncertain**, **conditional**, and **recommend against** can carry different practical meanings across settings.
