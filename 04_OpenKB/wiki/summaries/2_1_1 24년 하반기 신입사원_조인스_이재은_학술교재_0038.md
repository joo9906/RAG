# Summary: Joins — Efficacy and Indication Comparison

This document compares **Imotion®** and **Joins®** in terms of their approved use and reimbursement scope for degenerative joint disease, with an emphasis on the difference between a **knee osteoarthritis-only indication** and a **broader systemic reimbursement range**.

## Key point

- **Imotion®** is described as having an indication limited to **knee osteoarthritis**.
- **Osteoarthritis** can occur in many joints throughout the body, not only the knee, but also commonly in the **spine**, **hip**, and **finger joints**.
- **Joins®** is presented as being reimbursable for **degenerative joint disease across the whole body**, including examples such as **spinal osteoarthritis**.

## Main takeaway

The central message is that **Joins® has a broader reimbursement applicability than Imotion®**, making it relevant for patients with degenerative joint disease beyond knee-only osteoarthritis. This contrast highlights the importance of matching the drug’s coverage or indication to the affected joint location and the patient’s clinical presentation.
