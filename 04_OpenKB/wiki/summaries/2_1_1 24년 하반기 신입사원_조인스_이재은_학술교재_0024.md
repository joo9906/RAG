# Summary: 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0024

## Overview
This document summarizes a phase 3 clinical study of **JOINS®** in patients with **knee osteoarthritis (OA)**, comparing it with **diclofenac** for efficacy, safety, and tolerability.

## Study Details
- **Study type:** Randomized controlled trial ([[concepts/randomized-controlled-trials|RCT]])
- **Study objective:** Compare the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of JOINS® and diclofenac for the treatment of degenerative joint disease
- **Population:** Patients with knee osteoarthritis, **n = 249**
- **Intervention:** **JOINS 200 mg tid**
- **Comparator:** **Diclofenac SR 100 mg qd**
- **Duration:** **4 weeks**

## Endpoints
### Primary endpoint
- **VAS** (visual analog scale for pain)

### Secondary endpoints
- **Lequesne index**
  - knee pain
  - walking distance
  - activities of daily living
- **Patients’ global satisfaction score**
- **Investigators’ global satisfaction score**

## Key Findings
- In patients with knee OA, **JOINS 200 mg three times daily** showed **similar clinical efficacy** to **diclofenac SR 100 mg once daily** over 4 weeks.
- JOINS demonstrated **better safety and tolerability** than diclofenac.

## Main Takeaway
This study supports JOINS as a **non-inferior symptomatic treatment option** for knee osteoarthritis, with comparable short-term efficacy and potentially improved [[concepts/safety-and-tolerability|safety/tolerability]] relative to diclofenac.

## Reference
- Jung YB et al. *Am J Chin Med.* 2004;32(2):291-301.
