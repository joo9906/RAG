# JOINS: 1-Year Cartilage Protection Evidence in OA

## Summary
This document reports a clinical phase 4 osteoarthritis (OA) cartilage-protection result for JOINS®, based on a 1-year dGEMRIC assessment. The key finding is that the JOINS® treatment group maintained the dGEMRIC index over 1 year, while the placebo group showed a significant decrease, suggesting preservation of cartilage glycosaminoglycan (GAG) content.

## Key Findings
- JOINS® showed a cartilage-protective effect after 1 year of treatment.
- In the JOINS® group, the dGEMRIC index was maintained over the 1-year period.
- In the placebo group, the dGEMRIC index decreased significantly.
- The difference in dGEMRIC index change between groups was statistically significant.

## Clinical Meaning
The dGEMRIC index is used to evaluate cartilage GAG content, which reflects the status of proteoglycan components in cartilage. Because JOINS® preserved dGEMRIC values relative to placebo, the document presents it as evidence for [[concepts/cartilage-protection]] in [[concepts/osteoarthritis]] treatment.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/dgemric]]: MRI-based index used to assess cartilage GAG content.
- [[concepts/gag]]: Glycosaminoglycan, a major cartilage matrix component.
- [[concepts/osteoarthritis]]: The disease context for this cartilage-protection study.
- [[concepts/cartilage-protection]]: Therapeutic goal of preserving cartilage structure and composition.

## Reference
- Kim JI et al. *Knee Surg Relat Res.* 2017 Sep 1;29(3):217-224.
