# Joins Tablets Overview

## Summary
This document introduces **Joins tablets (조인스정)** as a **natural-product medicine / herbal combination drug** made from extracts of three medicinal plants: **Clematis mandsurica (위령선)**, **Trichosanthes kirilowii (괄루근)**, and **Prunella vulgaris (하고초)**.

It also explains the role of **marker compounds** used in quality management. For Joins, the identified marker compounds are **oleanolic acid** and **rosmarinic acid**, which are used to maintain consistent quality and efficacy.

## Key Points
- **Product type:** Herbal combination drug ([[concepts/herbal-medicine]])
- **Ingredients:**
  - Clematis mandsurica extract
  - Trichosanthes kirilowii extract
  - Prunella vulgaris extract
- **Quality control concept:** The product is standardized using **marker compounds** ([[concepts/marker-compound]])
- **Marker compounds named in the document:**
  - Oleanolic acid
  - Rosmarinic acid

## Concept Notes
### Herbal combination drug
Joins is presented as a **phytochemical-based pharmaceutical product** rather than a single purified active ingredient medicine. Its formulation relies on multiple plant extracts working together.

### Marker compound
A **marker compound** is a chemically identified constituent in the raw material that is designated for **quality control purposes**. In this document, marker compounds are used to help ensure product consistency across batches.

## Takeaway
The document’s main contribution is a concise description of Joins as a standardized herbal medicine and an explanation of how **marker compounds** support its quality assurance framework.
