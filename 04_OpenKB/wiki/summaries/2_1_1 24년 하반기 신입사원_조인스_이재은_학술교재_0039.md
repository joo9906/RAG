# Summary: 조인스

This document contrasts **JOINS®** with **Imotone®** in terms of approved indications, focusing on **rheumatoid arthritis (RA)** support for JOINS and the associated phase 3 clinical evidence.

## Key Point: RA Indication and Reimbursement

- **Imotone®** is noted as having an indication only for **knee osteoarthritis**.
- **JOINS®** can be prescribed with reimbursement for **rheumatoid arthritis (RA)**.
- The document emphasizes that JOINS has **clinical research evidence in RA patients**, distinguishing it from Imotone.

## RA Phase 3 Clinical Trial Evidence

The document restates the core findings of a **phase 3 randomized controlled trial (RCT)** comparing JOINS with **celecoxib** in RA patients.

### Trial Design
- **Study type:** RCT
- **Objective:** Compare pain relief and tolerability of JOINS® vs. celecoxib in patients with rheumatoid arthritis
- **Participants:** RA patients, **n = 183**
- **Intervention:**
  - **JOINS 200 mg tid**
  - **Celecoxib 200 mg bid**
- **Duration:** 6 weeks
- **Endpoints:**
  - **Primary:** VAS (pain score)
  - **Secondary:** rescue medication use frequency, **20% improvement response rate**

### Main Conclusion
- In RA patients, the **pain-relieving effect of JOINS was non-inferior to celecoxib**.
- **Tolerability was similar** between the two treatments.

## Why This Matters

This page supports the positioning of JOINS as a product with:
- a **rheumatoid arthritis indication**,
- **clinical trial evidence** in RA,
- and **comparative efficacy data** versus a standard NSAID comparator.
