# Summary: 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0009

## Overview
This document summarizes the treatment of **osteoarthritis (OA)**, focusing on the main goals of therapy, the role of **non-pharmacologic treatment**, common **drug therapies**, and the classification of OA medications into **SYRADOA**, **SYSADOA**, and **DMOADs**.

## Key Treatment Goals
The primary goals of OA treatment are to:

- reduce joint pain
- maintain joint function and prevent deformity
- improve the patient’s quality of life

These goals reflect a symptom-centered approach to managing OA and preserving daily function over time.

## Non-Pharmacologic Treatment
The document emphasizes that **non-drug therapy** is a core part of OA management.

Main examples include:

- exercise
- weight control
- muscle strengthening

These interventions are presented as essential foundational care in [[concepts/osteoarthritis-management]] and are important for long-term symptom control and functional support.

## Pharmacologic Treatment
The document lists several common medication approaches:

- **Topical agents**: NSAIDs and related preparations
- **Oral agents**: acetaminophen, NSAIDs, COX-2 inhibitors
- **Intra-articular injections**: hyaluronic acid, steroids

The document notes that current non-drug and drug therapies mainly target **symptoms**, not the underlying cause of OA.

## Limits of Current Therapy
A key point is that, so far, there is **no proven medication** that can prevent or reverse the degenerative structural changes associated with OA progression.

This distinction is important for understanding the gap between symptom relief and true disease modification in [[concepts/disease-modifying-osteoarthritis-drugs]].

## OA Drug Classifications
The document introduces three functional categories of OA therapies:

### SYRADOA
**Symptomatic Rapid Acting Drugs in OA**
- acetaminophen
- NSAIDs
- intra-articular corticosteroids

These agents are used for relatively fast symptom relief.

### SYSADOA
**Symptomatic Slow Acting Drugs in OA**
- glucosamine/chondroitin sulfate
- avocado/soybean unsaponifiables
- diacerin
- Joins®

These agents are associated with slower onset but longer-lasting symptomatic benefit.

### DMOADs
**Disease Modifying OA Drugs**
- drugs that may reduce or alter structural joint changes

The document highlights DMOADs as a theoretical or desired category rather than an established, proven option in current practice.

## Notable Drug Mentioned
The product **Joins®** is specifically included under SYSADOA, suggesting its use as a slow-acting symptomatic treatment in OA.

## Takeaway
Overall, the document presents OA treatment as a combination of **lifestyle-based care**, **symptom-relieving medications**, and an ongoing unmet need for **true disease-modifying therapy**. It provides a concise framework for understanding OA drug categories and their clinical roles within [[concepts/osteoarthritis]].
