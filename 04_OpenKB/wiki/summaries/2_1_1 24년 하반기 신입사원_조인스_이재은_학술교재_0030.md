# Summary: JOINS OA Cartilage Protection Phase 4 Study [JNS_028]

## Overview
This document summarizes a randomized controlled trial (RCT) evaluating the cartilage-protective effect of **JOINS®** in patients with **knee osteoarthritis (OA)**. The study compared **JOINS 200 mg three times daily** with **placebo** and used **MRI-based biomarkers** to assess structural changes over 12 months, with an additional 12-month extension.

## Study Design
- **Study type:** RCT
- **Population:** Osteoarthritis patients, **n = 69**
- **Intervention:** **JOINS 200 mg tid**
- **Comparator:** **Placebo**
- **Duration:** **12 months + 12-month extension**
- **Main evaluation tools:**
  - **dGEMRIC**
  - **Subarticular BME** (bone marrow edema)
  - **VAS**
  - **K-WOMAC**

## Key Purpose
The trial aimed to determine whether JOINS provides **clinical cartilage protection** in OA patients by comparing MRI-based outcomes against placebo. This makes the study relevant to [[concepts/osteoarthritis]], [[concepts/cartilage-protection]], and [[concepts/MRI-biomarkers]].

## Main Findings
- JOINS showed **superior clinical effect for cartilage protection** compared with placebo.
- The document emphasizes **MRI-based evidence** as the basis for assessing structural benefit, rather than only symptom relief.

## Interpretation
This study suggests that JOINS may have a **disease-modifying or structure-preserving effect** in knee OA, at least as reflected by imaging markers. The inclusion of both structural and symptomatic measures (dGEMRIC, BME, VAS, K-WOMAC) supports a broader evaluation of treatment effect.

## Reference
- Kim JI et al. *Knee Surg Relat Res.* 2017 Sep 1;29(3):217-224.
