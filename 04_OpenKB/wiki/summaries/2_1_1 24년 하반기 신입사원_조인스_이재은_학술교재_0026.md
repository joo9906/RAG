# 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0026

## Overview
This document summarizes a phase 3 osteoarthritis (OA) clinical trial comparing the adverse reaction rates of [[concepts/joins]] and diclofenac.

## Key Finding
- In the OA phase 3 trial, **JOINS® showed a significantly lower rate of drug-related adverse reactions than diclofenac**.
- The reported adverse reaction incidence was **19.2% (n=125)** in the JOINS® group versus **37.1% (n=124)** in the diclofenac group.
- The between-group difference was **statistically significant** (**p=0.017**, chi-square test).

## Clinical Relevance
- The document highlights the **safety advantage** of JOINS® relative to diclofenac in an OA study context.
- The result supports discussion of [[concepts/adverse-drug-reactions]] and [[concepts/osteoarthritis]] in comparative NSAID-style treatment evaluation.

## Source
- Jung YB et al. *Am J Chin Med.* 2004;32(2):291-301.
