# Summary

This document summarizes the **2-year safety results** of the OA cartilage-protection Phase 4 clinical trial for **JOINS**, comparing it with placebo.

## Main finding

JOINS® demonstrated a **safety profile similar to placebo** after 2 years of administration.

## Safety outcomes

In the study population:

- **JOINS group (n=38)**
  - Adverse events: **10 cases (26.32%)**
  - Drug-related adverse events: **3 cases (7.89%)**
  - Serious adverse events: **0 cases**

- **Placebo group (n=38)**
  - Adverse events: **12 cases (31.58%)**
  - Drug-related adverse events: **1 case (2.63%)**
  - Serious adverse events: **1 case (2.63%)**

## Interpretation

There were **no statistically significant differences** between the two groups in adverse events, drug-related adverse events, or serious adverse events. This supports the conclusion that [[concepts/safety_profile]] of JOINS is comparable to placebo in long-term use.
