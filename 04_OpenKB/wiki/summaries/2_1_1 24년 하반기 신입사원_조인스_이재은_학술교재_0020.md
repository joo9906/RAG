# 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0020

## Summary
This document summarizes the adverse reaction profile of **Joins tablets (조인스정)** based on a Korean post-marketing surveillance study conducted for reassessment in **osteoarthritis / degenerative joint disease**.

## Key findings
- Post-marketing surveillance period: **2001.07.10–2005.07.09**
- Study population: **5,962 patients**
- Overall adverse event incidence, regardless of causality: **3.02% (180/5,962)**
- Adverse events that could not be ruled out as related to the drug: **2.38% (142/5,962)**

## Main adverse reactions by system
### Gastrointestinal system
Most frequently reported reactions were:
- Heartburn: **0.82%**
- Dyspepsia: **0.57%**
- Epigastric discomfort: **0.32%**
- Gastrointestinal disorder: **0.32%**
- Epigastric pain: **0.20%**
- Constipation: **0.07%**
- Vomiting: **0.02%**

### General/systemic reactions
- Facial or hand edema: **0.37%**
- Headache: **0.05%**

### Skin and appendages
- Pruritus: **0.03%**
- Rash: **0.02%**

### Blood system
- Leukopenia: **0.03%**

### Psychiatric system
- Insomnia: **0.02%**

### Autonomic nervous system
- Palpitations: **0.02%**

### Hepatic/biliary system
- AST/ALT elevation: **0.02%**

### Central/peripheral nervous system
- Dizziness: **0.02%**

## Interpretation
The safety profile in this document suggests that **gastrointestinal adverse reactions** were the most common events observed during post-marketing surveillance of Joins tablets, while other reactions were infrequent and distributed across multiple organ systems.
