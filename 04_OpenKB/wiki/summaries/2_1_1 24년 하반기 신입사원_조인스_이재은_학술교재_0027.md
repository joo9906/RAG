# Summary: 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0027

## Overview
This document summarizes a randomized controlled trial (RCT) comparing **JOINS®** with **celecoxib** in patients with **rheumatoid arthritis (RA)** over **6 weeks**. The focus is on analgesic efficacy and tolerability.

## Study Details
- **Study type:** RCT
- **Disease area:** Rheumatoid arthritis ([[rheumatoid arthritis]])
- **Objective:** Compare the pain-relief effect and tolerability of JOINS® and celecoxib
- **Population:** RA patients, **n = 183**
- **Intervention:** **JOINS 200 mg tid**
- **Comparator:** **Celecoxib 200 mg bid**
- **Duration:** **6 weeks**

## Outcomes
### Primary endpoint
- **VAS** (visual analog scale) for pain

### Secondary endpoints
- Frequency of **rescue medication** use
- **20% improvement response rate**

## Main Finding
- In patients with RA, **JOINS showed pain-relief efficacy that was noninferior to celecoxib**.
- **Tolerability was similar** between the two treatments.

## Conclusion
The trial suggests that JOINS may be a viable treatment option for **pain management in rheumatoid arthritis**, with efficacy comparable to celecoxib and a similar safety/tolerability profile.

## Reference
- Song YW et al. *Clin Ther.* 2007 May;29(5):862-873.
