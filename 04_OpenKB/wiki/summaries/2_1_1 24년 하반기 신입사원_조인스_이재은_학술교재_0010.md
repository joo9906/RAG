# Summary: 2_1_1 24년 하반기 신입사원_조인스_이재은_학술교재_0010

## Overview
This document summarizes the **2019 ACR (American College of Rheumatology) guideline** for the management of **osteoarthritis (OA)** in the **hand, knee, and hip**. It highlights how treatment options are divided into **strongly recommended** and **conditionally recommended** categories, with emphasis on both **nonpharmacologic** and **pharmacologic** care.

## Key Points
- The guideline applies to **hand OA**, **knee OA**, and **hip OA**.
- Treatments are organized by recommendation strength:
  - **Strongly recommended**
  - **Conditionally recommended**
- The slide indicates major treatment categories such as:
  - **Exercise**
  - **Weight loss**
  - **NSAIDs**
  - **Intra-articular (IA) steroid injections**

## Clinical Themes
### Nonpharmacologic management
The guideline emphasizes conservative interventions as foundational OA care, especially **exercise** and **weight reduction** where appropriate. These approaches reflect the importance of lifestyle and functional improvement in chronic joint disease. See also [[concepts/nonpharmacologic-treatment]] and [[concepts/lifestyle-interventions]].

### Pharmacologic management
Medication-based options include **NSAIDs** and joint injections such as **IA steroids**, reflecting symptom-focused treatment for pain and inflammation. These treatments are presented as part of a broader OA management strategy rather than standalone solutions. See also [[concepts/NSAIDs]] and [[concepts/intra-articular-injection]].

## Main Takeaway
The document provides a concise overview of the **2019 ACR OA guideline**, showing that OA treatment is structured around a combination of **nonpharmacologic therapy**, **analgesic/anti-inflammatory medication**, and **targeted injections**, with recommendation strength guiding clinical decision-making. This connects to broader themes in [[concepts/osteoarthritis-management]] and [[concepts/rheumatology-guidelines]].
