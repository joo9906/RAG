## Overview
This document provides a basic introduction to **osteoarthritis (OA)**, focusing on its **etymology, definition, and pathophysiology**. It is a concise foundational note on one of the most common joint diseases.

## Key Concepts

### 1) Meaning of Osteoarthritis
- **Osteo** = bone
- **Arthro** = joint
- **-itis** = inflammation
- The term literally refers to inflammation involving bone and joints.

### 2) Definition of OA
- **Osteoarthritis (OA)** is the **most common form of arthritis**.
- It is also called:
  - **Degenerative arthritis**
  - **Degenerative joint disease**

### 3) Pathophysiology
- In OA, the **articular cartilage** covering the joint surface wears down over time.
- As cartilage is lost, the **underlying bone becomes exposed**.
- The **synovial membrane** around the joint becomes inflamed.
- These changes lead to:
  - **Pain**
  - **Joint deformity**
